# Plansom — Brand Pyramid

*Synthesised from the Functional POV, Emotional POV, USPs, Messaging Map and Positioning Framework.*

A brand pyramid climbs from the concrete (what the product is and does) to the abstract (the single idea the brand owns). Each tier is the reason the tier above it is believable.

```
                        ▲
                   ESSENCE
        "Put your effort where it pays off most."
              (Outcome discipline)
                   ─────────
                  PERSONALITY
      Disciplined · Direct · Intelligent · Calm in command
                 ───────────────
                  EMOTIONAL BENEFITS
   In control · Confident · Ahead · Free from wasted motion
                ─────────────────────
                 FUNCTIONAL BENEFITS
  Clear outcome focus · Highest-payoff prioritisation ·
  Visible blockers · Confident handoffs · Directed AI
              ─────────────────────────
            FEATURES / REASONS TO BELIEVE
  PlansomScore · Goal hierarchy · Bottleneck visibility ·
  Instant plans · Agent alignment & success checks
```

---

## Tier 1 — Brand Essence *(the peak)*

**Put your effort where it pays off most.**

> **Distilled essence:** *Outcome discipline.*
> The one idea Plansom owns: helping people — and their AI agents — decide what work deserves effort **before** activity becomes noise.

**Brand promise:** *More progress. Less wasted effort.*

**Category Plansom creates:** The **outcome-discipline engine for people and AI agents** — an AI-first planning *discipline layer*, not another task tracker.

---

## Tier 2 — Brand Personality & Voice

How Plansom shows up and sounds — the character behind the words.

| Personality | Voice / Tone |
|---|---|
| **Disciplined** — focused on what moves the outcome | Clear, ordered, focused |
| **Direct** — no fluff; names the issue plainly | Gets to the point; names the tension |
| **Intelligent** — smart by design, never gimmicky | Commercially sharp, never jargon-y |
| **Calm under pressure / in command** — control when it counts | Reassuring, grounded, confident |
| **Modern & forward-moving** — AI-first, decisive | Active and progress-oriented |

**Tone summary:** *Direct, disciplined and calmly ambitious — the voice of a system that keeps people and AI agents focused on the work that pays off most.*

**Do this, not that:** lead with outcomes (not generic PM copy) · name the tension (not vague complexity) · make discipline feel useful (not productivity guilt) · treat AI as capacity (not AI hype/theatre).

---

## Tier 3 — Emotional Benefits *(how it feels)*

Scored on Desirability × Ownability, three "hero" emotions lead — they're both wanted **and** ownable by Plansom in the AI era (rivals sell speed; Plansom owns direction and discipline).

**🏆 The hero three (lead here):**
1. **In control of where effort goes** — the core emotional prize.
2. **Confident handing work off** — delegation, made safe.
3. **Safe directing AI** — the AI-era differentiator.

**Supporting feelings:** Free from wasted motion · Disciplined, not overwhelmed · Ahead · Purposeful · Clear-minded · Unblocked · Capable with AI.

> **Emotional north star:** **Ahead** — *moving before the situation overtakes you.*
> The strategic ladder: **Fear** (falling behind) → **Promise** (discipline) → **Method** (put effort where it pays off) → **Payoff** (more progress, less wasted effort).

## Tier 4 — Functional Benefits *(what you get)*

Ranked by Desirability × Credibility. The top of this list is what's both wanted *and* provable today — lead with these.

| # | Functional benefit | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clear outcome focus** — work stays tied to what you're trying to achieve | ✅ Strongest / prove now |
| 2 | **Highest-payoff prioritisation** — know the next move that creates the most progress | High want · build proof |
| 3 | **Visible progress blockers** — see what's stuck before it slows you | High want · build proof |
| 4 | **Delegation-ready workflows** — hand work off without losing clarity | ✅ Strongest / prove now |
| 5 | **Instant plan structure** — turn goals & docs into a plan in minutes | ✅ Strongest / prove now |
| 6 | **Trusted agent delegation** — direct AI at real goals, safely | Differentiator · build proof |
| 7 | **Reduced wasted effort / repeatable decision discipline** | Supporting |

**Four functional pillars that organise everything:**
**Start with the outcome** → **See what's holding you back** → **Put effort where it pays off most** → **Keep people and agents aligned.**

---

## Tier 5 — Features & Reasons to Believe *(the base — what backs the promise)*

The concrete product proof that makes every tier above credible.

- **PlansomScore** — ranks actions by likely payoff (the signature proof point)
- **Instant plan generation** — goals & docs become a structured plan
- **Goal hierarchy** — outcomes nest into clear structure
- **Bottleneck visibility** — surface what's stuck, and why (+ stuck-work & attention signals, dependency tracking)
- **Best-practice task configuration** — clear ownership, steps and expected outputs
- **AI-agent alignment** — agents directed at real goals, with **success checks, permissions & escalation**
- **AI Plan · Manage · Execute** — connected planning through to completion

> **Proof discipline:** lead with what's demonstrable today (outcome focus, delegation-ready workflows, instant plans). Build evidence — PlansomScore methodology, before/after prioritisation, blocker demos, time-saved data, agent-delegation proof — **before** over-claiming highest-payoff prioritisation and directed AI.

## Supporting foundation

**Positioning statement**
> **For** high-output teams, operators and managers who feel effort is scattered faster than progress is made,
> **Plansom is** the AI-first planning discipline layer — the outcome-discipline engine for people and AI agents —
> **that** turns documents, goals and intent into outcome-led plans,
> **because** it shows what matters most, structures work for confident handoff, and gives humans and AI agents clear direction.

**Core tension it resolves:** *"We're working hard — but are we getting ahead?"*

**Target audience:** Teams, managers, founders, operators and knowledge workers who need to turn messy inputs (documents & intent) into focused, accountable progress — fast.

**Competitive frame:** Monday, Asana, ClickUp, Wrike, Notion manage *activity*. Plansom is the **discipline layer** that **decides · structures · directs** work before activity becomes noise. White space owned: **outcome-led effort discipline + safe AI direction.**

**Campaign / creative platform:** *"Discipline That Keeps You Ahead"* · bold territory: *"Command the Chaos."*

**The four USPs to build on:**
1. **Put Effort Where It Pays Off** (primary / lead promise)
2. **Delegate Work With Confidence** (most practical proof today — lead sales here)
3. **Give AI Agents Direction, Not Just Tasks** (biggest white space — build proof, then scale)
4. **See What's Blocking Progress** (everyday operational proof)
