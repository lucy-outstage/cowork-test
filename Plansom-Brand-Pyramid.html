Positioning statement

For high-output teams, operators and managers who feel effort is scattered faster than progress is made, Plansom is the AI-first planning discipline layer for people and AI agents that turns documents, goals and intent into outcome-led plans, because it shows what matters most, structures work for confident handoff, and gives humans and AI clear direction.

Core tension: "We're working hard — but are we getting ahead?"  ·  Campaign platform: Discipline That Keeps You Ahead  ·  Bold territory: Command the Chaos
