PLANSOM · GTM PILLAR 09 · REACH & CAPTURE INTENT

Traffic System

Plansom is an AI-powered planning platform that transforms ideas, tasks, and goals into clear, actionable plans.

How this answers “Who is ready to use Plansom today — and why?”

Search and intent-driven traffic are the purest readiness signal there is — someone typing the problem into Google, or asking an AI assistant, is telling you they are ready right now. This pillar captures that demand (SEO/GEO) and manufactures it (paid ads), routing both to trials.

Paid delivers ~200 trials/mo (LinkedIn ~62, Meta ~60, Google ~77) at $233–$480/trial — expensive against outreach, which is why organic SEO/GEO is the long-run margin play.

Tracking OS spec

  • Analytics: GA4 + a product-analytics tool (events across visit → signup → activation → paid).
  • UTM schema: source / medium / campaign / content enforced on every link.
  • Attribution: first + last touch, plus self-reported ‘how did you hear’ at signup.
  • Consent + privacy banner aligned to the cookies/privacy page (Pillar 02).

Build tracking before scaling spend — without it, budget is blind.
