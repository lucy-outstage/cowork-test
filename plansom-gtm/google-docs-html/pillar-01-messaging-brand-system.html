Messaging & Brand System

Plansom is an AI-powered planning platform that transforms ideas, tasks, and goals into clear, actionable plans.

How this answers “Who is ready to use Plansom today — and why?”

This pillar is where the answer is first written down. It converts a vague market into a short list of named segments, each paired with the specific trigger event that makes that segment ready now — new funding, a leadership change, tool sprawl, a missed quarter — and the pain that trigger creates.

Every other pillar inherits its targeting and its “why” from here. Messaging produces the hypotheses that Outreach and Traffic test in-market, and the language that Content expresses. If this is vague, the whole engine chases the wrong people at scale.

  • Outcome-centric — goals, KPIs and OKRs, tracked and repeatable (“win on repeat”).
  • Enterprise-safe by default — Azure, encrypted, SOC 2, GDPR/CCPA, no LLM training on your data.

Voice & tone

  • Simple & clear — plain language, no jargon; one idea per line.
  • Action-first — imperative, outcome verbs: simply done, smash goals, win on repeat.
  • Confident, not hypey — back every claim with proof (Microsoft, SOC 2).
  • Benefit before feature.
  • Words we use: simply, done, goal, plan, prioritise, track, agents, outcomes, on repeat, smash, share.
  • Avoid: corporate filler, feature dumps, unproven superlatives, and “to-do list” language except to position against it.
