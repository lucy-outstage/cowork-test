# Plansom GTM — Pillar Documentation

Two documents digesting the *Plansom GTM Hub 2.0* plan into an actionable, per-pillar overview.
Everything is organised around the core GTM question: **"Who are the people ready to use Plansom today — and why?"**

## Documents

1. **`Plansom_GTM_Pillar_Overview.docx`** — one page per pillar (max two).
   For each of the 10 pillars it answers *how that pillar gets us closer to the core question*,
   then lists its components, sub-components, key considerations and a build brief.
   Order: 1 Messaging & Brand · 3 Outreach · 4 Spin Sell · 5 Customer Journey ·
   6 Customer Advisory Board · 7 Accelerators & Events · 8 Partnerships & Directories ·
   9 Traffic · 10 Social · **2 Content System (placed last, most detailed)**.

2. **`Plansom_Content_Operating_System.docx`** — the dedicated deep-dive on Pillar 2 (Content),
   since it is by far the most detailed pillar (80+ assets across 10 component groups).
   Each sub-component has key considerations and a build brief, plus a map of how the
   Content OS feeds every other pillar.

## Framing

Each pillar is tagged by the role it plays in answering the core question — it either
**defines** the hypothesis of who is ready, **reaches** those people, **qualifies / converts**
their readiness into usage, or **expresses** the "why" so prospects can self-identify.

Figures cited in the documents (cost-per-meeting, conversion rates, trial volumes) are drawn
directly from the funnel and channel-OS tabs of the source workbook.
