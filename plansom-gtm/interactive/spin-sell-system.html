Pillar 04 · the human selling motion

Where “are they ready?” gets answered — live

SPIN — Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff — is a diagnostic method. It surfaces the prospect's real situation and pain, quantifies the cost of not solving it, and lets them articulate the value themselves. It converts the meetings Outreach books into activated trials, and disqualifies the not-ready quickly so effort lands where readiness is real.

The readiness bar

A prospect is “ready” only when all four are true. This is the qualify / disqualify line.

Trigger

An observable event is forcing the issue now — funding, a new leader, tool sprawl, missed goals.

Pain owner

The person on the call feels the coordination / goal pain themselves.

Authority / path

They can buy, or bring the buyer — roughly a 10-seat team decision.

Timing

Willing to start a trial and invite a team within two weeks.

Component 01

SPIN sell process documentation

The playbook every rep runs: the discovery framework, the qualification gates, the question bank, the 30-minute call shape, and the responses to the objections you'll hear most. Purpose — diagnose whether the prospect is genuinely ready and capture the “why” in their own words, then convert to an activated trial.

The SPIN method

Four stages, in order. Each one earns the right to the next — you don't demo until you've reached need-payoff.

S · Situation

Map the lay of the land

How they plan and track goals today, team size, tools, whether they're in Microsoft Teams. Keep it short — buyers hate feeling interrogated.

P · Problem

Surface the friction

Where it breaks down: planning, prioritisation, status updates, delegation. Let them name the pain in their words.

I · Implication

Make the cost real

What a missed or slipped goal actually costs. What a manager hour on admin vs outcomes is worth. This is where urgency is created.

N · Need-payoff

Let them sell themselves

“If a goal turned into a ready-to-share plan and agents handled some tasks — what would that free up?” They describe the win; you take notes.

Discovery → demo flow · 30 minutes

The standard first meeting. Don't skip to the demo before need-payoff.

0–5frame

Frame & confirm

Set the agenda, confirm the trigger, and check who's in the room.

5–15diagnose

SPIN discovery

Surface the pain and its implication. Most of the value of the call is here.

15–25show

Tailored demo

Their goal → a shared plan → an agent doing a real task. Only what they named — nothing else.

25–30commit

Need-payoff → start trial

Confirm the payoff in their words, start the trial, agree activation + team invite.

Component 03

Follow-up sequence

What happens after the meeting decides whether the trial converts. Speed-to-follow-up is the single biggest conversion lever after the meeting — templatise it, automate it, and trigger it the moment a meeting ends.

The rule: the Day-0 recap goes out within the hour, while the conversation is still warm. Everything below is a HubSpot sequence that fires on meeting-end; an agent drafts each touch for the rep to approve.

The 14-day cadence

Owner: the AE, assisted by agents. Every touch has one job and one clear next step.

Multi-threading & activation

Two things that quietly win or lose the deal.

Multi-thread the account

A single champion is a single point of failure. By Day 9, get a second contact in — ideally the buyer. Log every contact and their role on the deal.

Trial-activation nudges

A trial that isn't activated in week one rarely converts. Nudge toward the first real action: set a goal, invite the team, turn on one agent.

Component 05

Resource & capacity tree

The model that keeps meeting supply matched to selling capacity. If Outreach books more meetings than the team can run well, trials leak. Change the assumptions and the whole tree recomputes.

Assumptions editable

Reading it. The tree flows supply → capacity check → trials → wins → revenue. The verdict compares the meetings your current AEs can run against the meetings booked; a shortfall is leaked trials. All figures are illustrative until you set your real numbers.
