Where Glean agents would be most helpful

Glean shines where work depends on stitching internal knowledge across CRM, email, Slack, Gong, docs, wikis and tickets. The placements below are ranked by expected leverage.

1. Account 360 / Pre-call brief High

Stage: Prospecting Hypothesis → Outreach · augments Enzo & Jarvis
  • Stitches CRM history, prior email threads, Slack mentions, Gong calls, support tickets and exec news into a single brief.
  • Cuts AE/SDR prep time and surfaces "who has touched this account before" instantly.
  • Closes a real gap: Enzo/Jarvis build hypotheses; Glean supplies the lived institutional memory.

2. Technical docs / architecture / security RAG High

Stage: Technical Solution Selling · Technical lane
  • SE-grade answers from product docs, runbooks, KB, RFP responses, security & compliance library.
  • Drops POC and security-review cycle time; reduces "let me get back to you" loops.
  • Pairs with the SWOT/disqualification agent to back claims with citations.

3. Competitive battle-card retrieval High

Stage: Customer Evaluation · Commercial lane · augments Jeeves
  • Pulls current battle cards, win/loss notes, recent compete intel from Slack and CI tools.
  • Keeps Jeeves' persuasion grounded in the latest field intelligence, not last quarter's deck.

4. Similar-customer pain-pattern search Medium

Stage: Opp Creation & Qualification · Technical lane · augments Reginald
  • "Find 5 closed-won accounts with this pain + industry + size" across Gong, CRM notes, case studies.
  • Feeds Reginald better analogues so the pain→solution translation is grounded in real prior deals.

5. Content & talk-track retrieval for outreach Medium

Stage: Outreach & Early Exploration · Commercial lane
  • Surfaces the right one-pager, customer story, or analyst quote for a given persona/industry/pain.
  • Reduces reliance on tribal knowledge of "which deck do I send?"

6. Post-sale handoff brief generator Medium

Stage: Vendor Selection & Deal Closure → Onboarding · Commercial lane
  • Assembles a handoff doc from the deal record: pains, success criteria, technical context, stakeholders, MAP.
  • Reduces dropped context between AE/SE and CS/onboarding teams.

7. Routing rules & territory lookup Coverage gap

Stage: Account Routing & Sales Assignment · Technology lane
  • Today: no AI in this stage. Glean can answer "who owns this account / what's the ROE here?" from policy docs and SFDC.
  • Low-effort, high-frequency time-saver for SDRs and ops.

8. Onboarding playbook & ramp-plan agent Coverage gap

Stage: Onboarding · Technology lane
  • Today: no AI in this stage. Glean can surface the right onboarding playbook, prior ramp plans, and similar-customer launch timelines.
  • Closes the last-mile gap after Lurch's MAP execution.
