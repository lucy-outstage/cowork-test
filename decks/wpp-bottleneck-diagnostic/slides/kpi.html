One number, four parts.

  What we would propose
MetricPaying Open clients with no prior WPP relationship.
TargetA first number small enough to be honest about.
DateInside the current financial year.

Filled in with the leadership team. We would not set it from outside.

The right column is deliberately unquantified. We have no visibility of pipeline, pricing or capacity, so naming a target here would be a guess dressed as analysis. The point of the slide is that all four parts get agreed at once, and that one person owns it.
