03   The plan, inside Plansom

One goal, five workstreams, about ninety tasks.

W1

Data and compliance

Source, enrich, verify net-new, screen for CTPS every 28 days.

W2

LinkedIn motion

Twenty invitations a day per voice, assigned each evening for the morning.

W3

Phone motion

Two dial blocks a day, 75 dials each, from a screened list.

W4

Meetings and trials

Five discovery calls a day each, recorded, with the same eight questions.

W5

Measurement

Daily dashboard, weekly gates, transcripts coded as they land.

Every task has an owner, a day and an acceptance test. Roughly a third of them are agent work. Nobody writes a status report, because the plan is the status.

The full task tree is in the playbook, built and ready to run.

Point at the playbook here rather than reading the task list. The argument on this slide is structural: five workstreams, one owner per task, a third of the work delegated to agents, and no separate reporting layer.
