Where the work would live

The plan is already built. The task force would be invited into it.

Plan.

Actions, owners, dates and what done looks like — already written out, task by task.

Manage.

Standups and bottleneck reports assemble themselves. Nobody is asked for an update.

Execute.

Agents take the repetitive half — drafting, coding, assembling. Each has a named owner.

Nothing to roll out, nothing to integrate, and nobody has to learn a new way of working.

WPP is not being sold software here. They are being given somewhere to work for the duration. The footer answers the question an enterprise IT person would raise before they raise it.
