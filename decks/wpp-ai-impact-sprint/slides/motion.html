The motion

Two channels, and nothing else.

LinkedIn

WPP people inviting people, from their own profiles, with the note drafted for them the evening before. Sent by hand.

Telephone

Two callers working a screened list in focused blocks. Fewer people reached, better conversations.

The message

Brand control, not creativity. Three versions of it, split across the segment, to see which opens the door.

Every account is checked against WPP’s client records first. Anyone with a relationship in the last three years comes out of the list.

The footer is the line that protects WPP's existing book. Nobody in the current client base gets contacted, and without that check the test could not tell new money from money WPP already has.
