The shape of the work

From goal to answer.

Stage What happens What exists afterwards
DiscoverInterviews, baselines, current AI mapped against the goalCandidate goals, with the numbers behind each
DecideOne session to name the obstacle and agree the measureA signed one-page brief. WPP chooses whether to go on
BuildSegment verified, compliance cleared, channels readiedA plan running, and a task force in it
CalibrateLive at low volume, to measure the real ratesActual acceptance, reply and connect rates
RunFull volume, sized by what calibration showedConversations held, recorded and coded
ReviewThe result against the measureAn answer, with its confidence stated

Discover and Decide stand alone. WPP chooses at that point whether anything follows.

Calibration is the stage worth defending if challenged. Going straight to full volume against rates nobody has measured is how tests like this usually go wrong. One quiet period turns the largest unknown into a measured input.
