The proposal

Sell Open to someone who has never bought from WPP.

Goal

Paying Open Pro customers with no existing WPP relationship.

Obstacle

WPP is built for long relationships. These buyers want to start alone.

Measure

Qualified conversations per hundred approached, and trials started.

The obstacle is a structural fact, not a failing. Being built around long senior relationships is what makes WPP valuable to its largest clients. It is simply the wrong shape for someone who wants to begin on their own.
