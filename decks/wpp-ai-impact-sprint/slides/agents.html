Recommended for you, tested before they go live, and built from one sentence when you need a new one. No prompts to write and no project to set up.

Agents connect to email, calendar, Slack and call notes. Each one is tried on your last few items before it is trusted with new work.

The "who owns it" column is the reassurance. Nothing here is autonomous in a way that worries a brand-governance person — every agent has a named human owner, and the two that say "nobody" are reporting agents that cannot touch a client.
