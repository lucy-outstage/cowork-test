Two meetings a week for the sponsor. Fifteen minutes a day for everyone else. No status decks, because the plan is the status.

Red at a Friday gate has a pre-agreed action. Acceptance below 35% is a targeting problem; reply below 8% is a message problem; neither is fixed with more volume.

This is the slide that answers "how much of my people's time will this take?" — the honest answer is very little from most of them, and the pre-agreed red actions stop the Friday meeting becoming a debate.
