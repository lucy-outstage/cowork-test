The weekly review

What we do when it is not working.

If we see What we do that week
Few invitations acceptedRevisit the segment. The targeting is wrong, not the message
Accepted, but few repliesMove to the next version of the message. Not more volume
Replies that stallRead that week’s transcripts before changing anything
Few people answering the phoneRe-source the numbers. More dials will not help
Conversations booked but not heldSame-day reminders, before drawing any conclusion about demand
Buyers asking for bespoke termsRecommend stopping. The offer needs a person to sell it

Agreed with the sponsor before the motion starts, so a difficult week produces a decision rather than a discussion.

The last row is the important one. A sprint that ends in stop is a good result — it costs a short period and saves WPP from committing a year to something that does not convert. Saying that out loud signals we will not argue the numbers into a continuation.
