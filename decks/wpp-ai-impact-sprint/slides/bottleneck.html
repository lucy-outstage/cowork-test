Where we left the diagnostic

The product exists. The market exists. The route between them does not.

The product

Open Pro launched October 2025, built for buyers who cannot afford a full-service integrated agency. 75,000 internal users behind it.

The market

Roughly 1,600 clients already sit in WPP's tail, and a far larger population outside it has a brand to protect and no route to agency-grade discipline.

The missing piece

No motion to buyers who were never going to retain an agency, and no evidence of whether the demand is real.

This bottleneck needs no pricing reform, no product build and no restructure. It is the one that can be released inside a quarter — and tested in seven weeks.
