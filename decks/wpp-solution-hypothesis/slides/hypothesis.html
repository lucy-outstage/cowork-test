The hypothesis

Companies WPP has never served will buy Open on its own.

Without a pitch, without a retainer and without an agency attached. If that is true, Open has a market WPP has not counted. If it is false, that is worth knowing before anyone builds for it.

Everything that follows exists to prove or disprove this one sentence. Note what it does not claim: nothing about the largest clients, nothing about retention, nothing that asks WPP to change how it serves the accounts it already has.
