The honest part

What would tell us to stop.

The calls connect and nobody books.

The offer is wrong, not the channel.

They book and do not arrive.

Curiosity, not intent.

They arrive and want an agency attached.

Open does not sell alone, which is worth knowing early.

Agreeing the failure conditions before starting is what separates a test from an initiative. Each of the three is a useful answer, and the third would be the most valuable one to learn cheaply.
