# Realigning the WPP Work to the AI Impact Sprint

**Plansom · 6 October 2026**
*What the deck changes, and what we propose instead.*

---

## The finding

The AI Impact Sprint is **already a defined, priced product**: seven weeks, two phases, $25,000, built around **one goal, one obstacle, one measure**, run by four named senior people.

Everything we designed for WPP — The Open Door, the 9-week schedule, the 5.2 FTE team, the 100,000-account programme — **is not that product.** It is a go-to-market build. It would cost WPP millions and take eighteen months.

> **The good news: the WPP opportunity fits the real product almost perfectly, and the analysis we have done is most of Phase 1 already.**

---

## The mapping

### One goal

> **Acquire paying WPP Open Pro customers who have never bought anything from WPP.**

It is a single outcome, it is measurable, and it is the one goal where AI has the most leverage in WPP's business right now — because it is the only route to new revenue that does not wait on outcome-based pricing.

### One obstacle

> **WPP has no motion for reaching buyers who were never going to retain an agency — and no evidence that the demand exists.**

Rose named complexity as WPP's root cause. This is complexity's commercial consequence: a company organised entirely around large relationships, holding a product built for small ones, with no way to connect the two.

### One measure

> **Qualified meetings per 100 net-new accounts contacted, plus trials started, by the end of week seven.**

Signed off by the sponsor with a baseline of zero, because this motion does not currently exist.

## The seven weeks, applied to WPP

### Phase 1 · Focus · weeks 1–2 · $5,000

| Week | Standard | Applied to WPP |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Discover — interviews, metrics, current AI mapped. Session 1 chooses the goal | **Largely done.** We hold the diagnosis, the eighteen-month narrative, the Open adoption picture and the long-tail hypothesis. Week 1 becomes interviews and baselines, not research from scratch |
| **2** | Decide — Session 2 names the obstacle, agrees the measure | Confirm the obstacle and fix the measure with the Open Pro sponsor |
| **End of 2** | Your call — continue, or walk away with the outputs | Unchanged |

**This is the whole proposal.** Five thousand dollars, two weeks, and WPP can stop with the analysis in hand. For a company spending ~£300m a year on AI, that is not a decision that needs a committee.

### Phase 2 · Impact · weeks 3–7 · $20,000

| Week | Standard | Applied to WPP |
|---|---|---|
| **3** | Build — plan in Plansom, sources connected, agents set up | The outbound motion built: segment sourced and verified net-new, CTPS screened, LinkedIn and phone cadences live, the attribution spine connected |
| **4–6** | Run — weekly reviews, bottlenecks cleared, agents at work | The motion runs. Agents draft personalised outreach, code transcripts, generate standups and bottleneck reports |
| **7** | Review — result against the measure, next goal chosen | The answer: do net-new buyers take meetings, and do they start trials |

## Why Plansom, specifically, for this client

Three things line up unusually well, and the proposal should lead with them.

**One — the Microsoft match.** Cindy Rose came from Microsoft, where she was COO of Global Enterprise and before that ran Microsoft UK and Western Europe. The hypothesis we are proposing *is* the Microsoft model: high-touch for the largest accounts, self-serve platform for everyone else. **Antoine Boris is former global lead of Microsoft IP co-sell.** That is the same commercial architecture, and Plansom has someone who built it.

**Two — the diagnosis is already done.** Most sprints open with a blank page. This one opens with a validated eighteen-month diagnosis, a tested hypothesis and a channel model. Phase 1 starts from evidence.

**Three — the philosophy is the thesis.** *Simply done* is not decoration here. WPP's own CEO says complexity caused the underperformance; the UK Government paid £1.5bn for simplification; and the long tail is only servable if the motion is radically simple. **Plansom is selling the exact thing WPP has publicly diagnosed as its problem.**
