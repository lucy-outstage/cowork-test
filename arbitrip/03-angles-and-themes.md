# Angles & Themes — mapped to the promised scope

> The angle bank. Pull from here in the monthly planning session. Every angle traces back to
> a pillar (`01 §8`) and a pain row (`01 §5`). Mix evergreen POV with timely momentum.

---

## How the angles map to the monthly scope

The June 22 scope = **8 posts + 1 blog** per month:

- **Arbitrip page (4/month):** 2 TMC · 1 airline · 1 loyalty
- **Jonathan (4/month):** loyalty
- **Blog (1/month):** rotates — **airlines → loyalty → TMCs → reassess**, repurposed into one of the posts above ("one piece, two legs")

So each month you're drawing roughly: 2 TMC angles, 1 airline angle, 5 loyalty angles
(1 page + 4 Jonathan), and 1 blog angle from the rotation. The banks below are sized to feed
that comfortably.

---

## Theme 1 — Accommodation intelligence (flagship / cross-vertical)

The category POV. These work on the page and seed blogs. Big-idea fuel.

- "Accommodation is the most underleveraged asset in travel. Here's the math."
- The afterthought tax: what every travel business loses by treating hotels as a line item.
- What our cross-vertical data shows about how people *actually* book accommodation.
- Flights get the glory, hotels get the margin: where the real money sits.
- The "build vs. plug in" trap — why in-house booking tech rarely pays back.
- 1.5M properties is table stakes. The 87%-book-a-top-5 number is the real story (personalization > inventory).

## Theme 2 — TMCs / travel agencies (2 page posts/month)

Pain rows: lost accommodation revenue · no transparency · in-house too expensive.

- The OTA leak: the accommodation booking you lose the second a traveler books direct.
- "We'll just build it" — the real cost of in-house hotel booking for a mid-market TMC.
- Transparency gap: you can't optimize the accommodation spend you can't see.
- How a mid-market agency recaptures hotel revenue without rebuilding its stack.
- Margin you're giving away: rates up to 30% below market, in your own branded flow.
- Leisure isn't off-limits — capturing the trips your business-travel tooling ignores.
- Testimonial → angle: turn a client quote into a "here's what changed" mini-story.

## Theme 3 — Loyalty programs (1 page + 4 Jonathan = 5/month)

Pain rows: undifferentiated program · stalling activation · members only redeem for flights.

- Every loyalty program looks the same. Accommodation is how you stop blending in.
- The redemption nobody picks: why "flights only" is killing your engagement.
- Activation is stalling — give members a reason to actually log in and transact.
- What a multi-million-member program learned about travel redemption (Poalim Wonder lineage, told carefully).
- Banks, wallets, retail clubs: the travel benefit your members already want.
- Personalization beats points: 87% book a top-5 recommendation — apply that to redemption.
- Jonathan's POV series: a loyalty operator's take on differentiation, member behavior, what's overrated.

> Jonathan's 4 are loyalty-leaning and more *personal/operator-voice*. Carousels with text
> work well for his profile. Arbitrip can repost his best ones → "more loyalty on the feed
> without posting anything new."

## Theme 4 — Airlines (1 page post/month + blog rotation kickoff)

Pain rows: flat ancillary revenue · accommodation add-on "hard to execute."

- Ancillary revenue is flat. Accommodation is the line item you're not selling.
- The obvious add-on that's hard to execute — and what "done-for-you" actually looks like.
- Beyond the seat: capturing the rest of the trip's spend.
- Why accommodation is the highest-frequency ancillary an airline can add.
- Momentum angle: ride the new partnership *thematically* (tens of bookings) — **no partner names until sign-off.**

## Theme 5 — Founder / industry perspective (reserved, deferred)

For when Benny's profile activates. Don't force it yet, but bank the angles:

- Where travel's biggest hidden asset is heading (the cross-vertical vantage point).
- Lessons from building accommodation tech across 4 verticals.
- Contrarian takes on loyalty, OTAs, and "the unbundling of the trip."
