## Cadence & volume

- **Weekly:** 1 post on the **Arbitrip page** + 1 post on **Jonathan's** profile.
- **Monthly total:** **8 posts** + **1 long-form blog**.
- The blog is **repurposed** into a shorter LinkedIn post — *"one piece, two legs."*
- **Evergreen runs on top:** Sharlottes continues events, testimonials, and partnership
  announcements separately — those are *additional*, not part of the 8.

## The 8-post split

| Channel | Count | Focus |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Arbitrip page | 2 | TMC |
| Arbitrip page | 1 | Airline |
| Arbitrip page | 1 | Loyalty |
| Jonathan | 4 | Loyalty |

**Two-hits logic:** page posts can be reposted by Jonathan and vice-versa, so each strong
piece lands on multiple feeds without new production.

## Visual formats

- **Two formats per week:**
  - **One carousel** — text-only or simple abstract infographic (Claude/Canva-level).
  - **One single-image post** — infographic-style but simpler.
- **Personal profiles (Jonathan):** text carousels work well; **avoid overly corporate
  graphics**. No heavy "corporate" visuals on personal profiles.
- **Design split:** Sharlottes handles Canva-level work internally (templates exist, e.g.
  testimonials). External designer only for higher-complexity Arbitrip page posts.
- **Open question to test:** whether AI/Claude-generated visuals clear the Arbitrip brand
  standard. **Test first** on lower-stakes posts; keep the designer on standby for the rest.
- **Needed:** brand guidelines + asset folder (colors, fonts, logos, collected assets) —
  Sharlottes has the folder; to be shared before the first batch.

## Blog & long-form roadmap

- **1 long-form blog / month**, repurposed into a LinkedIn post.
- **Rotation (start with the most urgent):** **Airlines → Loyalty (broadly) → TMCs → reassess.**
  - Airlines first because of current momentum (new partnership, tens of bookings).
- **Monthly 30-min planning session:** Arbitrip brings known topics, Lucy brings ideas, agree
  next month's subjects together. Keep it dynamic — decide month-to-month.

## Later-stage (not in scope yet)

- **Newsletter** — start with the **TMC** segment (largest audience; not enough loyalty
  audience yet to segment a newsletter). Later, segment by vertical.
- **White papers.**
- **Full case studies** — expand a testimonial into a 2–3 page case study for prospects.

## Definition of "ready to post"

A piece is ready when it has: ✅ an audience + pain (`01 §4–5`) · ✅ a pillar (`01 §8`) ·
✅ a proof point (`01 §6`) · ✅ on-voice copy (`01 §7`) · ✅ on-brand visual within the
format split · ✅ confidentiality check passed (airline names). Then: weekly review/approval
before release.
