# What Story Is Arbitrip Embedded In Now?

> The point of this page: see the gap between the story the market *currently* tells about
> Arbitrip and the story we want it to own. The gap is the whole opportunity.

---

## The public story today (what the market sees)

Pulled from arbitrip.com, the segment landing pages, and external mentions (StartupHub,
ZoomInfo, CB Insights, eliteAI, partner PRs).

**The headline they're embedded in: "AI hotel-booking platform with great rates and great NPS, acquired by an Israeli travel group."**

- **Product-led framing.** The site leads with the *what*: "next-generation hotel-booking
  technology," 1.5M+ properties, AI personalization, rates up to 30% below market, branded
  experience. Strong feature story; thinner category story.
- **Segmented by audience already.** Dedicated pages for Corporations, TMCs/Travel Agencies,
  Loyalty Programs (and airlines in the mix). The *structure* for multi-vertical messaging
  exists — it just isn't tied together by a single big idea publicly.
- **Credibility-heavy.** 1,200+ clients, 1,000+ corporations, up to 98% NPS, 87% book a
  top-5 recommendation. The proof is there and it's good.
- **The acquisition narrative.** Founded 2015; acquired 2022 by the Talma travel group
  (reported $35–45M), operates as an independent brand. Teams in Israel, Ukraine, UK.
- **Loyalty pedigree, quietly told.** Began with Bank Hapoalim "Poalim Wonder" (2021), now
  multi-million-member programs across Asia, Europe, North America. Supply via HyperGuest.
- **Newest momentum:** a partnership with a leading Israeli travel/talent group driving tens
  of bookings — the spark behind pushing the airline vertical. (Specifics still confidential.)

**On LinkedIn specifically:** the page is currently kept alive with *evergreen* content —
event presence, testimonials, partnership announcements (Sharlottes runs this). It's
maintained, not narrated. There's no recurring professional/POV content telling the
strategic story. That's the missing layer.

## The strategic story we want to own (what we're building)

From the GTM foundation sessions, the real, differentiated story is **not** "we have a lot
of hotels." It's:

> **Accommodation is the most underleveraged asset in travel — and every vertical is paying
> for it.** TMCs lose it to OTAs. Loyalty programs can't differentiate without it. Airlines
> watch ancillary revenue stay flat without it. Arbitrip is the engine that flips
> accommodation from afterthought to advantage.

This reframes Arbitrip from "a hotel API vendor" to "the company with a point of view about
where travel's biggest hidden asset is going" — backed by a cross-vertical data vantage
point no single-vertical competitor has.

## The gap = the opportunity

| Dimension | Story now | Story we're building |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Frame** | Product / features ("AI hotel booking") | Category POV ("accommodation is underleveraged") |
| **Entry point** | "Here's our platform" | "Here's the money/loyalty/revenue you're leaving on the table" |
| **LinkedIn** | Evergreen: events, testimonials, partnerships | + Weekly professional POV content across 3 verticals |
| **Voice** | Corporate, capable, a little flat | Human, sharp, *The Hustle*-style — still professional |
| **Proof** | Stats stated | Stats *in service of* a pain→shift narrative |
| **People** | Brand page only | Brand page + Jonathan (loyalty) now; Benny (founder authority) later |

Closing this gap is exactly the H1 mandate: keep the evergreen layer, and add a weekly POV
layer that tells the accommodation story three ways and compounds on the page + Jonathan's
profile.

## Three "portals of entry" into the market

A useful way to hold the multi-vertical story together — three doors, one house:

1. **TMCs / travel agencies** — *bread and butter.* Largest audience, deals already flowing (Inga's team). The volume engine.
2. **Loyalty programs** — *the connector.* The vertical that ties banks, fintech, retail, and airlines together; strongest proof heritage. Jonathan's turf.
3. **Airlines** — *test & experiment.* Newest, longest cycle, most lucrative; riding current momentum. Handle partner specifics with confidentiality.
