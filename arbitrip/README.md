## The one-paragraph version

Arbitrip's public story today is "AI hotel-booking platform, great rates, great NPS,
acquired by Talma." That's a *product* story. The strategic story we're here to build is
a *category* story: **accommodation is the most underleveraged asset in travel** — and
every vertical (TMCs, loyalty programs, airlines) is leaving money, differentiation, and
engagement on the table because of it. We tell that one idea three ways, in a voice that's
professional but human (think *The Hustle*, not a corporate deck), and we let the LinkedIn
page + Jonathan's profile compound it weekly.

## H1 scope at a glance (from June 22)

- **8 LinkedIn posts / month** — 4 on the Arbitrip page (2 TMC, 1 airline, 1 loyalty) + 4 on Jonathan's profile (loyalty).
- **1 long-form blog / month**, repurposed into a LinkedIn post ("one piece, two legs"). Rotation: **airlines → loyalty → TMCs → reassess.**
- **2 visual formats / week**: one carousel (text / simple infographic) + one single-image post.
- **Monthly 30-min planning session** to pick next month's topics.
- Budget anchor: **~$1K/month** (8 posts + 1 blog + visuals).
